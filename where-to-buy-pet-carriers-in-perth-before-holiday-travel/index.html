Where to Buy Pet Carriers in Perth Before Holiday Travel

By Maddie SorensenSep 4, 2026Pet Carriers & Backpacks5 min read

Short answer: in Perth you can buy a pet carrier from big-box pet stores, local independent shops, and a few specialist travel-gear retailers, but stock thins out right before school holidays, so shop early. As a Perth groomer and mobile fitter I send clients to different stores depending on whether they need a cheap cabin bag tonight or a fitted, airline-ready carrier with advice. Know your pet's measurements and your airline's limits before you walk in, and you will leave with the right box instead of a refund later, which is the last thing you want with a trip already booked.

Start with measurements, not the shelf

Before any shopping trip, weigh your pet and measure length from chest to tail base and height at the shoulder. Retailers across Perth stock the same size charts, so the number that fits is the number that fits everywhere. Take a photo of your pet standing for reference, because memory lies about size.

This step saves a second trip when the holiday is already booked, and it lets the shop assistant pull the right box without guessing from a vague description over the phone.

Big-box and chain stores

The large pet chains around Perth carry the widest range and often the cheapest soft carriers. They are reliable for a standard cabin bag and usually have stock even midweek. The trade-off is less one-on-one fitting help, so know your sizes going in, or you may stand confused in front of a wall of near-identical bags.

  • Wide range and steady stock.
  • Good for standard cabin sizes.
  • Less personal fitting advice.
  • Open late, handy for workers.

Independent and suburban shops

Smaller Perth stores and suburban pet shops often stock fewer units but give better guidance, and some will help you test the fit with your own pet. For an awkwardly sized cat or a nervous animal, that advice is worth the slightly higher price, because a wrong carrier is no bargain at any discount.

I send first-time flyers to the independents on purpose, because the ten minutes of fitting chat prevents the heartbreak of a refused carrier at the gate two weeks later.

If you are flying, the airline's own pet-page dimensions should sit in your phone notes while you shop, because the right size is the one that boards, not the one that looks roomy in the aisle. I have watched too many holiday travellers buy a lovely carrier that simply will not fit the seat, then scramble for a replacement the night before. A two-minute check against the airline's numbers saves that panic, and most Perth stores will happily steer you once they see the limit.

Specialist and last-minute options

A few travel-gear and reptile-or-bird style shops carry airline-ready soft bags too, though stock is limited and worth a call. Market stalls and pop-up stands appear in summer but vary wildly in quality, so treat them as a last resort rather than your primary plan for a flight.

Beat the holiday rush

In the weeks before Christmas and the January break, popular sizes sell out. I tell clients to buy three weeks early and keep the receipt, because plans change. If you are flying, confirm the carrier against your airline before the shops close for the break, since a closed store will not exchange your box.

For sizing help, our pet carriers and backpacks guides list what to measure, so you walk in knowing the number rather than guessing at the peg.

Finally, do not underestimate the value of a store with a real return desk you can walk into. Online-only buys are cheap until the size is wrong the night before a flight, and then you are stuck. A Perth shop with stock and a counter means you can swap the box the same day, which is worth a few extra dollars when the holiday depends on it going smoothly.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should I buy before a Perth holiday?

Shop at least three weeks ahead, because popular carrier sizes sell out before Christmas and the January break. Buying early also leaves time to train your pet and return the box if the fit is wrong, which holiday queues will not allow.

Do local shops help with fitting?

Many independent Perth stores will help you check the fit, and some welcome your pet for a trial. Chains are faster but give less guidance, so bring your measurements and photos to be safe either way, and ask before assuming they will fit your animal.

Can I get a carrier same-day in Perth?

Usually yes from a big-chain store, though holiday weekends thin the shelves. Call ahead to confirm your size is in stock rather than driving across town and finding an empty peg, because nothing ruins a trip like a missing carrier.

What if I buy the wrong size?

Keep the receipt and return it within the store's window, ideally unused with tags on. Measure your pet first to avoid the loop, and check the airline limit so the replacement is the right one rather than a second mistake.

Are market stalls worth it?

Seasonal markets can be a last-minute save, but stock and quality vary and warranty support is thin. For a flight, prefer a retailer with a clear return policy and a known brand you can trust, because the airline will not bend its rules for a pretty unknown bag.
