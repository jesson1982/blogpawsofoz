Waterproof & Breathable Pet Pens for Aussie Weather

By Sophie BennettJul 22, 2026Pet Playpens8 min read

Short answer: choose a pen with a waterproof coated floor and genuinely breathable mesh, because Wally's coastal Perth life swings from cooking footpaths to summer storms in one afternoon. Our soft pen runs a lightweight aluminium-style frame that wipes clean and never rusts like steel, which is what salty air and caravan trips ask of the gear. You should check the weave before a campsite night, since a fine mesh keeps flies and sandflies out while coarser mesh lets bugs through.

Living near the Perth coast means I have learned to respect the weather in a way I never did growing up. One moment the sun is cooking the footpath, the next a summer storm rolls in off the ocean and the footpath is underwater. When you share a flat with a Cavoodle puppy and a rescue kitten, the last thing you want is a pen that turns into a sauna or a sponge. That is why the two features I care about most are a waterproof dog playpen build and a genuinely breathable pet pen design, because Australian conditions ask both of those things at once.

Why our weather is a special case

Australian pet owners deal with a wider swing than most product descriptions admit. Inland, the heat can be brutal and shade is precious. On the coast, where Wally and I spend most weekends, the breeze is lovely until it isn't, and the humidity creeps in. Then there is the famous sudden rain: the kind that appears while you are at the beach and soaks everything you left out. A pen that only does one job, either keeping water out or letting air through, will let you down half the time. The trick is finding gear that does both without costing a fortune.

I also rent, which adds a wrinkle. I cannot drill a fixed run into the courtyard, and I cannot leave a heavy metal cage outside to rust. So my pen has to be portable, washable, and kind to the landlord's flooring. The soft foldable style ticks those boxes, provided the fabric is up to the climate.

Waterproofing that actually holds up

When I say waterproof dog playpen, I mean the base and lower walls should shrug off a downpour and a wet puppy without soaking through. The floor of our pen has a coated layer that wipes dry, so when Wally splashes his bowl or a storm blows in under the awning, the water sits on the surface instead of soaking into the carpet back at the flat. That matters in a rental, because a stained rug is a bond deduction waiting to happen.

Be careful with the word waterproof, though. A fully sealed pen with no ventilation would trap heat, and that is dangerous. The sweet spot is a water-resistant base with breathable upper walls, so rain stays out at the bottom while air moves freely up top. I learnt this the hard way with a cheap pop-up tent that kept the rain off but cooked Wally within twenty minutes. Waterproof is only good when it is paired with airflow.

Breathable panels for hot days

This is where a breathable pet pen earns its keep. The mesh sides on our soft octagonal pen are the feature I rate highest in summer. Air flows through from every panel, so even on a still afternoon Wally is not sitting in still, stale air. Miso, who tolerates heat even less than the dog, will happily lie near a mesh panel where the breeze finds her. Good mesh also means you can see your pet and they can see you, which keeps them settled when the neighbours' footstep noises travel down the hall.

Not all mesh is equal. The fine weave on a quality pen keeps flies and sandflies out on a coastal night, while the coarser budget mesh lets insects through and snags easily. If you camp, that difference is the line between a peaceful evening and a scratchy, buzzing one.

The pattern is consistent: lean on mesh whenever it is safe, and lean on the coated base whenever water is around. You rarely need both at full strength at the same time, which is why a soft pen you can reconfigure beats a rigid one you cannot.

Washable fabric is non-negotiable

Puppies are messy. Wally has managed to combine mud, seaweed and a half-eaten sausage in a single afternoon on the coast. A washable fabric is the only thing that keeps the pen from becoming a permanent smell in the flat. Ours unzips and goes in the machine on a gentle cycle, then air-dries by the balcony door. Miso's pen gets the same treatment after she decides the bed is a scratching post.

For renters this is gold. A pen that smells of wet dog is the kind of thing that follows you to the next inspection. A pen you can wash on a Sunday and have fresh by Monday is the kind you actually keep using. I would not buy any indoor pet gear that cannot be cleaned easily, and a breathable pet pen with a washable cover is the standard I hold everything to now.

Coastal camping with the pen

Our caravan trips are where the weatherproofing gets a real test. Salt air is rough on metal, which is another reason I prefer the soft aluminium-framed pen over a heavy steel cage that would rust in a season. The fold-flat frame wipes down with a damp cloth, and the coated floor handles the dew that settles on the annex floor overnight. On the one trip where the rain came sideways, the waterproof dog playpen base kept Wally's bed dry while everything else in the annex needed wringing out.

The breathable sides mean I can leave him safely contained while I cook, without worrying he is overheating in the enclosed space. That peace of mind is worth more than any fancy feature, honestly.

What I would tell a first-time pet parent

If you are choosing your first pen and the Australian climate is part of your life, do not get distracted by the tallest walls or the brightest colour. Ask two questions: can water be wiped off the base, and can air move through the sides. If the answer is yes to both, you have a pen that will survive a Perth summer and a coastal storm alike. Everything else, the pockets, the colour, the carry bag, is a bonus on top of those two foundations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is a waterproof dog playpen safe in hot weather?

Yes, provided it is also breathable. A waterproof base keeps water out at the bottom, while mesh upper panels let air move through. Never fully seal a pen, as trapped heat is dangerous for pets.

What makes a pet pen breathable?

A breathable pet pen uses mesh or ventilated panels so air flows through the sides. Our soft octagonal pen has mesh on multiple panels, giving cross-breezes even on still coastal evenings.

Can I use the pen outdoors in the rain?

The coated base handles rain and splashes, and the lower walls resist a shower. For heavy storms, set the pen under an awning or annex and keep the top open for airflow rather than wrapping it tight.

How do I clean a pen after the beach?

Shake out the sand, wipe the coated floor with a damp cloth, and machine-wash the removable fabric on a gentle cycle if your model allows. Air-dry fully before folding it away.

Will the mesh keep insects out?

A fine mesh weave keeps flies and sandflies out on a coastal night, while cheaper coarse mesh lets bugs through. Check the weave before you rely on it at a campsite.

Does a soft pen rust near the sea?

Our soft pen uses a lightweight aluminium-style frame that wipes clean and does not rust like steel. That makes it a sensible choice for salty coastal air and caravan trips.
