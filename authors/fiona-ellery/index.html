Fiona Ellery

Rescue Foster Carer · Ballarat, VIC · 4 posts

Fiona has fostered more than forty dogs and cats for Ballarat rescue groups since 2016 — the shutdown ones, the chewers, the staffy with the bad hip and the kitten raised in a laundry. Every guide she writes starts with the same question: what does this animal need in its first fortnight? She tests playpens, calming beds and settling setups in her own loungeroom before recommending them.
