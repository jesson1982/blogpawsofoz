Ruth Alderton

Retired Cattery Manager · Sydney, NSW · 4 posts

Ruth managed a boarding cattery in Sydney for eleven years, with upwards of thirty cats in residence at peak season. She knows which beds survive industrial washing, which toys hold up to strange cats, and how a new cat reads a strange room. Her guides are written for multi-cat households, where everything you buy has to work for three opinions at once.
