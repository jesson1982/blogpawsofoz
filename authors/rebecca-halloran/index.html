Rebecca Halloran

Pet First Aid Instructor · Gold Coast, QLD · 4 posts

Rebecca teaches pet first aid short courses on the Gold Coast, after a decade of seeing what heat, hooks, stings and bad travel setups do to dogs and cats on the way to the vet. Her guides cover the safety side of gear: restraint that doesn't restrict breathing, car cabins in a Queensland summer, and what every owner should be able to do before help arrives. She writes checklists and explains risks — she never diagnoses.

Credentials & experience

  • Pet first aid instructor, Gold Coast, QLD
  • Regular exposure to animal emergencies through course teaching
  • Writes safety checklists and travel-safety guides — no diagnosis or treatment advice
