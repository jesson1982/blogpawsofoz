Tom Riley

Adventure Dog Writer & Road-Tripper · Darwin, NT · 4 posts

Tom and Scout, his Border Collie, cover about twenty thousand kilometres a year across the Top End — camping, four-wheel-driving and testing exactly how long a dog will tolerate the back seat before the complaints start. He reviews travel gear the only honest way: on corrugated roads, in build-up-season humidity, with a dog who would rather be on the roof rack.
