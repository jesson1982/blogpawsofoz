Where to Buy a Dog Playpen in Australia (Amazon, PETstock, Kmart)

By Sophie BennettJul 21, 2026Pet Playpens7 min read

Short answer: compare Amazon, PETstock and Kmart on return rules and delivery time, then buy a foldable pen you can actually send back if it does not fit the flat. Keeping Wally and Miso in mind the whole time, I chose the size that fits both zones, with a litter tray at one end and the dog bed at the other. Here's the detail west-coast renters like me need: Paws of Oz ships its foldable line across Australia, including to Perth, so ordering direct avoids the long wait.

When I first went looking for a pen for Wally and Miso, I expected to walk into one shop and leave with the right thing. Instead I found a sprawl of options across Amazon Australia, the big pet chains, and the cheaper department stores, each with different prices, return rules, and delivery times. As a Perth renter watching my spending, I wanted something foldable, affordable, and not a hassle to send back if it did not fit the flat. Here is the straight comparison I wish someone had handed me before I started clicking around.

Where I started my search

I began with a list of what mattered: it had to fold flat, suit both a puppy and a kitten, and cost less than a vet bill. That simple list ruled out a lot of bulky fancy units straight away. From there the real question was not which store existed, but which store gave me the right mix of price, speed, and peace of mind on returns. Writing the list down first stopped me from being pulled in by the cheapest listing that would never have worked in my rooms.

Amazon Australia

Shopping on Amazon Australia is convenient, and the range is enormous, which is exactly why the search term dog playpen amazon australia pulls up so many results. You can read dozens of reviews in an evening and often get quick delivery if you are in a metro area. The catch is that quality varies wildly between sellers, and a pen that looks sturdy in photos can arrive flimsy. I lean on the star ratings and the real photos from buyers, and I always check the return window before ordering, because a marketplace listing is only as good as the seller behind it.

PETstock

PETstock is a familiar name for Australian pet owners, and the upside is that you can see the pen in person and talk to staff who handle pet gear daily. Prices sit in the mid range, and you get the comfort of a local return process if something is wrong. The trade-off is that the foldable range can be narrower than what you find online, so you may not get the exact size or shape you wanted for a small flat. For a first-time buyer who wants to touch the product first, though, the in-store confidence is worth something.

Kmart

Kmart is where many renters start because the price is hard to beat. You can grab a basic foldable pen on a routine shopping trip, and for a tight budget that is genuinely useful. The honest limitation is that the materials are lighter and the sizes are limited, so a Kmart pen may suit a small cat or a young puppy more than a full-grown dog. For a first pen on a shoestring, it does the job; for the long term, you may outgrow it and find yourself comparing stores all over again.

Specialty stores and buying direct

Beyond the big names, there are specialty pet stores and the brands that sell direct from their own websites. This is where Paws of Oz sits, offering the foldable playpen line as a direct option with local support. Why a pet enclosure foldable option suited me

For a renter, the single most useful feature is pack-down. A pet enclosure foldable design disappears into the wardrobe, travels to the campsite, and leaves no mark on the property. That flexibility matters more to me than a few extra features I would never use. Whatever store I bought from, foldability was the deal-breaker that kept the flat liveable, because a pen that cannot be put away is a pen that ends up cluttering the one spare corner I have.

What to check before you buy

Before you spend, check the footprint against your room, confirm the height suits your animal, read the return policy, and weigh the packed size if you plan to travel. A cheap pen you cannot return is no bargain, and a heavy one you cannot store will end up on the footpath. Match the pen to your actual space and your actual pet, not to the prettiest listing, and the right store becomes obvious once the noise is stripped away.

My eventual choice

After all that comparing, I ended up with a direct-order foldable pen from Paws of Oz for everyday use, and I keep an eye on Kmart for a cheap spare at the caravan. The mix gives me a reliable main pen and a backup without overspending, which is the renter-friendly balance I was after. Buying direct also meant I could ask a question about sizing before purchase, which the big marketplaces do not always make easy.

A note on patience

The temptation is to buy the first pen you see at midnight. I nearly did. Waiting a day to compare sizes and return policies saved me from a unit that would not have fit through the balcony doors. A little research up front is the cheapest part of the whole purchase, and it is the step that turns a random order into a pen you will actually keep and use for years.

Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently asked questions

Is Amazon Australia a good place to buy a playpen?

It is convenient with a huge range, which is why so many people search dog playpen amazon australia first. Just read buyer photos and the return window, since quality differs between sellers.

What is the cheapest option for a renter?

Kmart is usually the lowest price and handy for a first pen, though the materials are lighter. For a longer-term foldable pen, a direct order often gives better value than the very cheapest unit.

Should I buy in store or online?

In store, at PETstock or Kmart, lets you see the build and return locally. Online, including Amazon and direct brands, gives wider choice. I mixed both: a main pen direct, a cheap spare in store.

Are foldable pens worth the money?

For a renter, yes. A pet enclosure foldable design stores in a wardrobe and travels, so you actually keep using it. A rigid pen you cannot put away tends to become clutter.

Can one pen suit both a cat and a dog?

Mine does. I keep the litter tray at one end and the dog bed at the other, with mesh between them. Choose a size that fits both zones, which is where the medium or large really helps.
