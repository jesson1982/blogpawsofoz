Sharing a Window Perch in a Rental: Ending the Cat Stand-Offs

By Sophie BennettSep 2, 2026Cat Window Perches5 min read

Short answer: two cats can absolutely share one window perch in a rental, but they rarely share it at the same time. Ours worked once we stopped treating it as a single bed and started treating it as a rostered spot, mounted with no-drill suction cups that leave the frames untouched. One perch, two shifts, zero drill holes, and both cats get their window time without the stand-offs that used to happen on the sill.

Why one window caused a war

Our apartment has exactly one window with a decent view: a street-facing pane about 1.2 metres wide, with a courier, a lemon tree and a rotation of butcher birds. Both cats wanted it. The younger one would body-block the sill, the older one would stalk past and knock her off, and the sulking that followed could eat an entire evening.

Every multi-cat household runs on a resource map. In a rental, that map is compressed because you can't drill in shelves or bolt up a climbing wall. The perch had to earn its place without changing the property, and it had to feel fair to two cats with very different temperaments.

The setup that finally worked

After one failed cheap hammock and a lot of trial and error, the setup that stuck was boring and cheap:

  • One wide suction-cup hammock on the main pane, cleaned and pressed on properly
  • A low second option: a padded cushion on the window-side arm of the sofa, level with the sill
  • A folding step stool between the two so the older cat could get up without jumping
  • Felt pads under everything that touches the frame, so nothing scratches

The trick was giving them two window experiences instead of one. The hammock is the prize spot; the sofa perch is the fallback that means nobody has to fight for the glass.

The roster the cats actually follow

I'd love to claim I trained them to a schedule, but cats do this themselves if the resources are right. Morning sun belongs to the older one, who rises with the birds. Afternoon belongs to the kitten, who likes heat and drama. Evenings split naturally because one of them migrates to my lap anyway.

What I did add was feeding near the sofa perch in the morning, so the older cat started her day on the lower level instead of guarding the hammock. Within a fortnight the ambushes stopped. There's still a stare-down most days, but it resolves in seconds instead of escalating.

Keeping the rental safe

Everything here is reversible. The hammock comes off the glass in ten seconds, the suction marks wipe off with a damp cloth, and the stool lives in the cupboard between inspections. I keep the original packaging and receipts in the same folder as the lease, partly for the bond and partly because re-pressing the cups every few weeks has become routine. When sharing doesn't work

If your two cats are actively hostile rather than merely competitive, one window will keep being a flashpoint no matter what you mount on it. Space the options further apart, put a visual barrier like a bookcase between the two levels, and let them use the window at genuinely different times. If tension shows up as spraying or blocking each other's food and litter, that's a bigger resource problem than a perch can fix, and a chat with your vet or a behaviour consultant is worth it. Most pairs, though, just need a second option and a little patience. Ours did.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can two cats sit on a window hammock at the same time?

Sometimes, if the platform is wide and the pair get along, but most hammocks are rated and shaped for one cat. Treat it as a single seat with a queue, and provide a second, lower spot nearby so waiting doesn't mean conflict. The queue only works if both cats have somewhere comfortable to wait.

Will suction cups damage a rental window?

Applied to clean glass and removed properly, no. The risk is residue from cheap cups or marking on painted frames, so keep cups on the glass itself, wipe the pane before fitting, and peel cups off slowly at an angle. Any slight ring usually polishes out with glass cleaner before inspection day.

How do I stop my cats fighting over the window?

Add options rather than policing the one they fight over. A second perch at a different height, feeding one cat away from the window, and letting each cat claim a time of day all work. Blocking each other repeatedly is a resource-sharing problem, so widen the gap between the spots before anything else.

What weight rating do I need for two cats?

Even if they take turns, buy for your heaviest cat plus a generous margin, because landing cats hit the platform with more force than their standing weight. A rating well above your biggest cat is cheap insurance. Check the cups weekly, because a rating means nothing if the suction has dried out.

Can I put a perch on a rented apartment's sliding door?

Usually yes, if the door glass is flat, unrestricted and you don't block the track. Suction cups hold well on sliding door glass, and it's often the biggest sun-facing pane in a rental. Just confirm the door still opens freely and the cups sit clear of any film or coatings that might lift.
