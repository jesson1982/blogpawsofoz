About the Paws of Oz blog

Paws of Oz is an Australian pet supplies store. This blog exists because the product pages on their own don't answer the questions people actually type into Google — how big is it really, will it fit my ute, does it survive a Staffy, and is it worth the money.

Every guide here is written in the first person by someone who owns the animal in question. Our writers live in different parts of the country, deal with different weather, and own very different dogs and cats. Where we don't know something, we say so rather than guessing.

What we cover

  • Dog Leads & Walking Gear — Everyday walks, training lines and the gear that survives an Australian summer.
  • Dog Harnesses — Sizing charts, fit checks and honest notes on what actually stops a puller.
  • Pet Carriers & Backpacks — Bubble vs mesh, weight limits, padding and escape-proof zips.
  • Calming & Anxiety — Pheromone vs essential oil, fit, longevity and when to call the vet.
  • Pet Apparel — Measuring properly, reading UPF claims and dressing a dog who hates clothes.
  • Beds & Mats — Memory foam vs fibre, waterproof liners and beds for seniors.
  • Dog Car Seat Covers — Utes, SUVs and hatchbacks, from someone who drives dirt roads daily.
  • Pet Playpens — Apartment-friendly, fold-flat and genuinely escape-resistant setups.
  • Cat Window Perches — No-drill installs, summer mesh vs winter plush, multi-cat windows.
  • Cat & Dog Toys — Durability by weight, puppy teething and keeping indoor cats busy.

A note on advice

Nothing on this site is veterinary advice. Our writers are pet owners, trainers and vet nurses sharing personal experience, not clinicians giving diagnoses. If your pet is unwell, injured, or behaving in a way that worries you, please see your vet.
