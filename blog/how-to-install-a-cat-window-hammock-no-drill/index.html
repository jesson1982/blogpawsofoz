How to Install a Cat Window Hammock (No Drill)

By Sophie BennettJul 11, 2026Cat Window Perches6 min read

Sophie & the Fur Babies — suction-cup cat window hammocks for renters & sun-loving cats

The single best thing about a suction-cup cat window hammock is that you can put it up without a single screw. As a Perth renter, that is the deal-breaker for me. My lease says no holes in the walls, and I intend to get my bond back, so anything that needs a drill is a non-starter. When I fitted Miso's first perch, I was nervous it would slide straight off, but it held, and it still holds months later. This is the exact no-drill method I used, written so you can follow it on a lazy Sunday afternoon. I am not a tradesperson, just a careful first-time pet parent who read the instructions twice.

What you need before you start

Keep it simple. You need the hammock, a clean cloth, a little warm water, and optionally a hairdryer. That is it. No tools, no brackets, no trip to the hardware shop. I also clear the floor below the window so that if anything does drop, nothing breaks and no one is under it. Wally the puppy got banished to the other room during my first install because he thought the suction cup was a toy.

Prep and install at a glance

I like a checklist because it stops me rushing. Here is the prep-and-install table I wish I had on day one. Run through it top to bottom and the whole job takes about ten minutes, plus a short wait for the suction to settle.

StepWhat to doWhy it matters
1. Pick the windowChoose a smooth, clean, non-textured glass pane your cat already watchesTexture or film breaks the seal; the right window is the one your cat chose
2. Clean the glassWipe with warm water and a lint-free cloth, then dry fullyDust and oil are the enemy of suction
3. Warm the cupsQuick hairdryer pass on the suction rims (optional but helpful)Warm rubber forms a better grip on cool glass
4. Wet the rimDab a little water on each suction edgeA damp seal holds far stronger than a dry one
5. Press and lockPush each cup flat against the glass, then engage the lock leverFlat contact squeezes the air out for a real vacuum
6. Test gentlyPress down with your hand before letting the cat upConfirms the hold without risking your pet

Step-by-step, slowly

Start with the window. Miso already stared at the north window, so that is where hers went. Give the glass a proper clean. I thought mine was clean until I actually wiped it and the cloth came away grey. Dry it, then warm the suction rims with a hairdryer for a few seconds. Wet the very edge of each cup with a finger, line the hammock up level, and press from the centre outward so no air is trapped. Flip the little lock levers if your model has them. Step back, admire it, then test the hold with the flat of your hand. Only once it feels solid do I let Miso investigate.

Getting your cat to use it

Some cats leap up at once. Miso took two days and a sprinkle of catnip to trust the height. I helped by placing a favourite toy on the bed and sitting nearby so she read my calm as permission. Do not lift your cat onto it and drop them; let them choose. If they are nervous, leave it mounted and let curiosity do the work. Within a week she was hopping up on her own every afternoon.

Where the weight line sits

I want to be honest about limits, because a failed mount is dangerous and a misleading claim is worse. Our twin-suction hammock is a great fit for small and medium cats up to about 15 kg. I will not say it holds a heavy large breed, because it is not built for that. If your cat is large, please use a reinforced four-suction steel-cable model instead.

Looking after the hold

Suction is not fit-and-forget. Every couple of weeks I press the cups to check they are still suctioned, and if a corner has lifted I re-wet and re-press. In Perth's summer heat the glass expands a little, so a quick monthly check keeps things safe. If you move house, the perch comes off with a fingernail under the rim and leaves no mark, which is exactly why renters love it.

Frequently asked questions

Frequently asked questions

How do you install a cat window hammock without drilling?

Clean the glass, warm and lightly wet the suction rims, press each cup flat from the centre out, lock the levers, and test the hold with your hand before the cat uses it. No tools are required at any stage.

Do the suction cups hold on a rental window?

Yes, on smooth clear glass they hold well once seated properly. They leave no holes or marks, which is why they suit renters who need their bond back.

How long does installation take?

About ten minutes of active work plus a short settle. Most of that is cleaning the window properly, which is the step people skip and then regret.

Can a large cat use a suction hammock?

A cat over about 15 kg should use a reinforced four-suction steel-cable model. We do not claim our standard twin-suction hammock holds heavy large breeds.

Why did my hammock fall off?

Usually dust, a textured or filmed pane, or a dry rim. Re-clean the glass, wet the rim, and press flat. If it keeps dropping, the window surface may not be suitable.

How often should I check the suction?

Every couple of weeks, and after any hot spell. A quick press on each cup confirms the seal. Re-wet and re-seat if you feel any lift.
