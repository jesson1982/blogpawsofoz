Puppy Teething Toys: Saving Your Shoes in Week One

Puppy Teething Toys: Saving Your Shoes in Week One

Davo · Cluster F · primary: puppy teething toys

Friends with a new puppy asked me what to buy before week one, and my answer surprised them: not much, but the right few things. A teething puppy doesn't need twenty toys — it needs relief for sore gums and an alternative to your footwear.

Teething is a phase; damage doesn't have to be

Puppies teeth until roughly six months, and the urge to chew peaks when the adult teeth settle. Soft-edged rubber soothes gums; frozen items numb them; and a cool carrot does a surprisingly good job on a budget. The strategy is redirection — every time jaws find a shoe, swap it for the toy, calmly, fifty times a day if needed.

Age stageReach forAvoid
8–12 weeksSoft rubber, frozen clothHard chews — baby teeth
3–5 monthsPuppy-rated rubber, cool carrotAdult-tough rubber
5–6 months+Graduate to firmer chewsLeaving toys with glued parts

Buying tip for the online shop: get two of whatever works. Teething toys lead hard lives, get lost under lounges, and you'll want a dry one while the other's in the freezer.

Frequently asked questions

When do puppies stop teething?

Around six months, when adult teeth settle — chewing peaks just before.

Are frozen toys good for teething?

Yes — cold soothes gums. Try a frozen wet cloth or carrot.

Can puppies have adult chew toys?

Not yet — puppy-rated softness protects baby teeth.

How do I stop my puppy chewing shoes?

Manage (shoes away) plus redirect to a teething toy, calmly, every time.

How many teething toys do I need?

Three or four in rotation, including a spare while one's frozen.
