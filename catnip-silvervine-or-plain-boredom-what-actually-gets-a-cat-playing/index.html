Catnip, Silvervine or Plain Boredom? What Actually Gets a Cat Playing

By Hannah WrightJun 30, 2026Cat & Dog Toys2 min read

Short answer: if the kicker flops at home, the toy wasn't the problem — genetics was, since roughly a third of cats lack any catnip response. Here's the fix: reach for silvervine, which catches the cats catnip misses, and rotate the toys you own fortnightly so the 'new' one lands every time. My boy rolls on his like it owes him money while his sister walks past, and silvervine was the only thing that got her playing.

People are always surprised when I tell them catnip doesn't work on every cat. Roughly a third of cats carry no response to it at all — genetics, not personality. My own boy rolls on a catnip mouse like it owes him money; his sister walks straight past it. So if a "catnip toy" has flopped in your house, the toy wasn't the problem.

Three ways to spice up a toy

Catnip is the classic — dried leaves sewn into kickers and mice, and the response (rolling, drooling, bliss) fades after ten minutes or so. Silvervine is the newer arrival: a natural plant that seems to catch cats catnip misses, including many of the non-responders. Then there's plain novelty — a toy that crinkles or rolls can grip a cat with no herbs at all.

The trick nobody mentions is rotation. Cats habituate fast — a toy that thrilled them Monday is furniture by Friday. I keep a biscuit tin of catnip toys in the cupboard and swap them fortnightly; the "new" toy lands every time.

Frequently asked questions

Is catnip safe for cats?

Yes — it's non-addictive and harmless. Let them enjoy it and walk away when they're done.

Why does my cat ignore catnip toys?

About a third of cats lack the gene for a response. Try silvervine instead.

How often should I give catnip?

A couple of times a week keeps the effect strong; daily use dulls it.

What are silvervine sticks?

Chewable sticks from the silvervine plant. Many cats who skip catnip respond to them.

Do kittens respond to catnip?

Usually not until around three to six months of age. Plain play works better for kittens.

Can a cat have too many toys?

Out at once, yes. Rotate a small set and keep the rest hidden.
