The Best Window Spots for Cats in Small Perth Apartments

By Maddie SorensenSep 2, 2026Cat Window Perches5 min read

In a small Perth apartment, the best window for a cat perch faces east or south: the east pane serves up warm morning sun and bird traffic, and the south stays comfortable through a summer that turns every north and west window into a furnace by mid-afternoon. Orientation decides this city more than view does, because we get the hottest afternoons of any capital and glass amplifies them. Here's how to read your apartment's windows before you mount anything.

Why orientation matters in Perth

Perth's summer sun runs high and long, and afternoon sun from the north-west bakes west-facing glass hard. A window that feels lovely in May can hit temperatures in February that make the pane itself unpleasant to touch. Cats self-regulate and will move when hot, but a perch with no cooler alternative becomes furniture they ignore for three months, which is the actual failure mode: not danger, just a very expensive shelf the cat only uses in winter.

The fix is picking the window by its worst season, not its best one, and giving the perch a job in both.

The four orientations ranked

East is my default recommendation. Morning sun without the afternoon bill, plus the morning is when the birds and the neighbourhood cats are out, which is the show your cat actually wants to watch. South is the steady option, never spectacular, never a problem.

Glare, heat and glass you can manage

A few cheap fixes make a marginal window workable:

  • Sheer curtains or light window film on the hot panes cuts glare and heat without blocking the view entirely
  • A pot plant outside or on the sill breaks up reflections that spook some cats
  • A bird feeder hung just out of feline reach turns any window into the best one
  • Check the glass by hand on a 38°C day before trusting the perch in summer
  • If the apartment has air conditioning, note where the cool air actually lands, because the cats will

One warning on film: if you rent, check the lease before applying anything permanent to glass, and keep suction cups clear of film edges so you're not peeling it when the perch comes off. Our cat window perch guide has more on renter-friendly mounting.

Birdwatching beats a pretty view

Owners pick windows by view; cats pick them by movement. A ground-floor pane onto a courtyard with sparrows, skinks and passing dogs outperforms a floor-to-ceiling city view, every time I've watched cats choose. In the small apartments I visit around Perth, the winning window is usually the kitchen or lounge window facing the street trees, not the balcony door facing the skyline. If you're torn between two windows, sit at each for ten minutes at cat-eye level and count the moving things. The busier window wins.

Setting up two windows

If the apartment allows it, a summer perch and a winter perch beat one compromise. Mount the hammock on the east or south window for the warm months, and move it to the north or west window from around May, when the winter sun is gentle and every cat in Perth goes looking for it. This seasonal rotation costs nothing and it's the single biggest upgrade I suggest to clients whose perches have gone unused. If you can only do one window, choose east and add a washable pad so it's comfortable year-round, and position a second, low-level resting spot nearby for the hottest weeks. The cats will sort out the rest; they always do.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which window should I put a cat perch on in Perth?

East-facing is the best all-rounder, with warm morning sun and bird activity, and south-facing is the most comfortable through summer. North and west windows become too hot from late spring, though they're excellent from May to August. If you only have a hot window, plan to shift the perch or shade it in summer.

Is it dangerous for cats in a hot Perth apartment?

A perch beside hot glass is uncomfortable rather than dangerous if the cat can leave freely, but an apartment that stays above 30°C indoors is a genuine heat risk for any pet. Ensure shade, water and cool flooring are always available, and check the glass and fittings by hand on hot days before expecting the cat to use the spot.

How do I stop glare and heat on a west-facing window?

Light window film or a sheer curtain cuts most of the heat load, and an external shade or awning beats internal ones because it stops the sun before the glass. Check your lease before applying anything permanent. Realistically, a west window works as a winter perch, so plan a seasonal move rather than fighting February.

How do I attract birds to my cat's window safely?

Hang a feeder outside the window, positioned so cats can't reach it even if the window opens, and keep it stocked with seed suited to local species. Planting native shrubs nearby helps too. The cat gets the show from behind glass, the birds stay safe, and everyone's entertained. Just place it where the cat can see it clearly from the perch.

My cat ignores the perch. Could the window be the problem?

Often, yes. A window with no movement, heavy glare or uncomfortable heat gets ignored. Test by sitting at the window at cat-eye level and counting moving things over ten minutes, and try relocating the perch to a busier pane before blaming the cat. Seasonal changes matter too, so a perch abandoned in summer often revives in winter.
